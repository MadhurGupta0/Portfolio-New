# ExperienceItem

One role: when, what, for whom, and what came of it.

## You provide

- `role`, `org`, `period`.
- `summary`: one sentence on what you owned.
- `points`: two or three outcomes, each a full sentence with its evidence.
- `techStack`.
- `slot`: draws the entry dashed and muted, as a place still to be filled.

## Rules

- Newest first.
- `period` uses an en dash and four-digit years: "2024–2026", "2025–present".
- A point states an outcome and the fact that proves it: "Billing across Apple, Google Play and Razorpay unified behind one entitlement model", not "Worked on billing".
- Take `slot` off when the entry is real. Never publish a slot.
