# ContactBlock

The foot of every page: the email address at title size, one button that copies it, and where else to look.

## You provide

- `email`, and optionally `lead`, `links`, `location`, `resumeHref`, `colophon`, `id`.

## Rules

- The address is shown in full and is itself a mail link. There is no contact form.
- "Copy email" is the page's one primary button. Its label reads "Copied" for a moment once it has worked.
- `lead` is one sentence saying the fastest way to reach you.
- Give `resumeHref` once a résumé exists. The button reads "Download résumé".
