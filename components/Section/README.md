# Section

One section of a case study: the heading hangs in the left column, the prose runs at reading width on the right.

## You provide

- `title`: the heading.
- Children: paragraphs, lists, inline `code`, a `CodeBlock`, a `KnownGaps`.

## Rules

- A case study runs: the problem, the approach, one section per decision, verification.
- Title a decision section with the decision: "Two dependencies, on purpose". Not "Dependencies".
- Two or three paragraphs. A fourth usually means the section holds two decisions.
- Below 900px the heading sits above the prose.
