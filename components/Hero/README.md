# Hero

The top of the home page: who this is, one real system drawn, and how to reach them.

## You provide

- `statement`: one sentence with the name in it.
- `lead`: one or two sentences of specifics.
- `stages`, `flowLabel`, `caption`: the schematic of one real system, and the line that names it.
- `facts`: role, location, email, links.

## Rules

- The statement says what gets built, in plain words, and ends with a full stop. No adjectives about yourself.
- The email address is in the title block, in full, so it is on screen without scrolling.
- The schematic is the most characteristic system in the portfolio, and its caption links to that case study.
- Four to six stages, so the line holds on one row. Below 900px it stands on end.
- One hero per site. Other pages open with `CaseHeader`.
